How to use digital parking permits

Bayou Landing HOA — print this page and keep it by your phone.

To add or change your car

  1. Go to the parking website and choose I live here.
  2. Choose your address and type in your 6-digit PIN.
  3. Choose Add a car or Replace this car.
  4. Type your plate, color, make, and model, then choose Save my car.

Parking rules

  • Orange zone (main Ann St between the gates): you must have a permit.
  • Green zone (in front of homes): not patrolled.
  • Driveways: never towed.
