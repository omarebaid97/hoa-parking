Parking rules, in plain English

Orange zone

The main stretch of Ann St that connects both gates, plus the spots in front of the Lofts-facing units. This is patrolled.

You must have a visible permit here. A car with no valid permit in the orange zone {% if tow_approval_required %}needs Board/PMG approval before it can be towed.{% else %}may be towed.{% endif %}

Green zone

The Ann St stretches directly in front of homes. This is not patrolled.

A car here is only towed if a resident asks for it and shows photo ID or something proving their address.

Driveways

Private driveways are never towed.

Car permits

  • Each unit can have up to {{ cap }} car permits.
  • A permit is tied to your unit and one specific car. It cannot be given to someone else.
  • If you get a different car, replace the old permit — the old one turns off automatically.

Guest passes Proposed — pending Board approval

Not official policy yet. If approved, residents would be able to create a temporary pass for a visitor's car.
